# Enemy rosters

Each region has its own species. Encounters rotate through the listed roster; the final stage contains the listed guardian. Grassland 1's opening stage remains Slimes and Slashlings only. Swarm creatures spawn in groups of six.

Summoners produce three local creatures rather than generic Slimes. Movement and attack archetypes are shared where useful, while regional species have their own silhouettes and colors. Bosses retain 10× area health and control resistance.
