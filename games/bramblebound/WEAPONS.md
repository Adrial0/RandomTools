# Weapon catalogue

Warrior, Rogue, Ranger and Reaper receive seven weapons per tier: fire, ice, poison, lightning, an early physical weapon, a stronger late physical weapon without an ability, and one special. The late physical weapon has 25% higher minimum and maximum AT than the strongest other option. Mage has one physical option in tiers 1, 3 and 5. Priest and Summoner have six options per tier; Bard has five support instruments. Retired IDs remain usable in existing saves but are excluded from shops and drops. Clear a weapon’s source area to buy it.
