# Weapon catalogue

Each tier belongs to one zone. Clear the listed source area to buy a weapon. Weapon drops start on stages 3–5 of their source area. Original weapons and item IDs are retained. Physical bows gain one arrow per tier; legacy specialty bows keep their established arrow counts.
