# Current class roster

Existing numeric class and equipment IDs stay stable for saved parties. Swordsman becomes Warrior, Boxer becomes Rogue, Archer becomes Ranger, Spearman becomes Reaper, Gunner becomes Bard, and Whipper becomes Summoner. Mage and Priest retain their names.

Warrior swords and Reaper scythes use sampled blade segments over a swing. Rendering uses those same segments. Every contacted enemy can take one basic hit per swing; terrain clips contacts. Rogue dagger strikes remain single-target. Airborne or dragged characters cannot strike.

Reaper: 42 base range and 40–50 AGI. STR adds one minimum and maximum AT. DEX heals 0.5 LP per enemy struck by a basic swing. INT charges weapon abilities. Special attacks and damage-over-time do not trigger the DEX healing.

Bard: Lutes fire piercing lines, harps spread three weaker notes, horns fire broad short-range pulses, and flutes fire longer-reaching piercing notes. All have 55–65 base AGI and notes travel 35 units beyond their target (limited to targeting range plus 35). Each STR grants allies +1 flat AT and reduces affected enemies' damage by 0.5; each DEX grants allies +1% attack speed and makes enemies take 0.25 additional physical basic-hit damage. Damage-over-time and magic hits do not benefit from physical vulnerability. Enemy damage is at least 1. Each Bard applies a separate three-second contribution; that Bard's subsequent notes refresh its contribution. Different Bards and Priest auras stack. Harp volleys apply effects, heal, damage and charge MP once per target/attack as appropriate. INT charges musical abilities: Encore fires a non-charging bonus volley; Sustaining Melody makes the next notes last six seconds; Crescendo makes the next effects 50% stronger; Restoring Chord makes the next notes heal each crossed ally for 12 LP. Terrain blocks every pattern. Existing Bard item IDs map to the new musical instruments and abilities.

Summoner: grimoires define skeleton, spirit, or golem groups. Maximum count is base count times (1 + floor(INT / 10)). STR adds 10% minion damage per point; DEX adds 10% minion maximum health per point. Neither attribute increases attack speed. Minions follow their owner and engage within a 150-unit leash, are valid enemy targets, and return after a four-second delay (seven for golems). Summons are replaced when the grimoire or its sockets change and removed when the owner dies or leaves the stage. Elemental grimoires trigger their ability every five minion attacks, without the former Whipper activation multiplier.

Mage uses staves. Priest uses holy symbols, keeps its enemy-damaging/ally-buffing aura, and retains 80–90 base AGI. Weapons, fixed portraits, shop names, class choices, and stat readouts reflect the new roster.
