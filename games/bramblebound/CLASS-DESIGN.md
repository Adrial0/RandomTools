# Current class roster

Existing numeric class and equipment IDs stay stable for saved parties. Swordsman becomes Warrior, Boxer becomes Rogue, Archer becomes Ranger, Spearman becomes Reaper, Gunner becomes Bard, and Whipper becomes Summoner. Mage and Priest retain their names.

Warrior swords and Reaper scythes use sampled blade segments over a swing. Rendering uses those same segments. Every contacted enemy can take one basic hit per swing; terrain clips contacts. Rogue dagger strikes remain single-target. Airborne or dragged characters cannot strike.

Reaper: 42 base range and 40–50 AGI. STR adds one minimum and maximum AT. DEX heals 0.5 LP per enemy struck by a basic swing. INT charges weapon abilities. Special attacks and damage-over-time do not trigger the DEX healing.

Bard: Lutes fire piercing lines, harps spread three weaker notes, horns fire broad short-range pulses, and flutes fire longer-reaching piercing notes. All have 55–65 base AGI and notes travel 35 units beyond their target (limited to targeting range plus 35). Each STR grants allies +1% AT and reduces affected enemies' damage by 0.5; each DEX grants allies +1% attack speed and makes enemies take 0.25 additional physical basic-hit damage. Damage-over-time and magic hits do not benefit from physical vulnerability. Enemy damage is at least 1. Each Bard applies a separate one-second contribution; that Bard's subsequent notes refresh its contribution. Different Bards and Priest auras stack. Harp volleys apply effects, heal, damage and charge MP once per target/attack as appropriate. INT charges musical abilities: Encore fires a non-charging bonus volley; Sustaining Melody makes the next notes last two seconds; Crescendo makes the next effects 50% stronger; Restoring Chord makes the next notes heal each crossed ally for 12 LP. Terrain blocks every pattern. Existing Bard item IDs map to the new musical instruments and abilities.

Summoner: each cast creates one creature, lasting 15 seconds or until killed. Spirit grimoires have 55–65 AGI, skeleton grimoires 85–95, and golem grimoires 140–160. Cast interval is average AGI / 30 / (1 + INT × 0.02), additionally affected by haste. STR adds 10% minion damage per point; DEX adds 10% minion maximum health per point. Creature attack intervals are separate: spirits 1 second, skeletons 1.5, golems 2. Deaths do not reset casting progress or trigger individual respawns. Casting progress can finish in the air, but spawning waits until the summoner is grounded and released. Minions follow their owner within a 150-unit leash and are valid enemy targets. Changing gear removes old summons and resets casting; owner death or leaving a stage removes summons. Elemental grimoires trigger their ability every five minion attacks.

Mage uses staves. Priest uses holy symbols, keeps its enemy-damaging/ally-buffing aura, and retains 80–90 base AGI. Weapons, fixed portraits, shop names, class choices, and stat readouts reflect the new roster.
