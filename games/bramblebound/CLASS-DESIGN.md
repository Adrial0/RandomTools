# Current class roster

Mage: no MP. Every elemental staff projectile activates its weapon effect on impact, with no additional basic hit. Plain staves fire a basic magic bolt. INT adds 1 minimum and maximum AT per point; STR increases range and DEX increases attack speed. Spell damage uses the cast's AT, including support bonuses. Fire distributes it over ten pulses and poison over four ticks; other effects use the spell AT per affected target. Terrain blocks spell projectiles. Mage MP bars and weapon MP readouts are hidden.

Rogue: 110 base HP. STR adds minimum and maximum AT. DEX recharges an automatic dodge in `6 / (1 + DEX * 0.05)` seconds. Only a successful dodge starts the timer; incoming damage during recharge does not reset it. Direct enemy attacks and projectiles can be dodged, but boss specials bypass it without consuming readiness. INT grants 1% basic-attack critical chance per point (up to 100%) and normal MP gain; critical basic attacks deal double damage. The stat panel shows recharge time, readiness and critical chance.

The final areas of each three-area region (indices 2, 5, 8, 11, 14 and 17) feature five smaller guardians. Each has 24% of the ordinary boss HP and 55% of its damage, normal-sized artwork and collision bounds, and a single telegraphed dart special. Boss control resistance remains. All five must die before the exit countdown starts. Boss XP is divided between them, with the boss equipment drop roll awarded on the last death. These encounters still contain at most three enemy species.

Existing numeric class and equipment IDs stay stable for saved parties. Swordsman becomes Warrior, Boxer becomes Rogue, Archer becomes Ranger, Spearman becomes Reaper, Gunner becomes Bard, and Whipper becomes Summoner. Mage and Priest retain their names.

Warrior swords and Reaper scythes use sampled blade segments over a swing. Rendering uses those same segments. Every contacted enemy can take one basic hit per swing; terrain clips contacts. Rogue dagger strikes remain single-target. Airborne or dragged characters cannot strike.

Reaper: 42 base range and 40–50 AGI. STR adds one minimum and maximum AT. DEX heals 0.5 LP per enemy struck by a basic swing. INT charges weapon abilities. Special attacks and damage-over-time do not trigger the DEX healing.

Bard: Lutes fire piercing lines, harps spread three weaker notes, horns fire broad short-range pulses, and flutes fire longer-reaching piercing notes. All have 55–65 base AGI and notes travel 35 units beyond their target (limited to targeting range plus 35). Each STR grants allies +1% AT and reduces affected enemies' damage by 0.5; each DEX grants allies +1% attack speed and makes enemies take 0.25 additional physical basic-hit damage. Damage-over-time and magic hits do not benefit from physical vulnerability. Enemy damage is at least 1. Each Bard applies a separate one-second contribution; that Bard's subsequent notes refresh its contribution. Different Bards and Priest auras stack. Harp volleys apply effects, heal, damage and charge MP once per target/attack as appropriate. INT charges musical abilities: Encore fires a non-charging bonus volley; Sustaining Melody makes the next notes last two seconds; Crescendo makes the next effects 50% stronger; Restoring Chord makes the next notes heal each crossed ally for 12 LP. Terrain blocks every pattern. Existing Bard item IDs map to the new musical instruments and abilities.

Summoner: each cast creates one creature, lasting 15 seconds or until killed. Spirit grimoires have 85–95 AGI (3 seconds), skeleton grimoires 145–155 (5 seconds), and golem grimoires 290–310 (10 seconds). Cast interval is average AGI / 30 / (1 + INT × 0.02), additionally affected by haste. STR adds 10% minion damage per point; DEX adds 10% minion maximum health per point. Creature attack intervals are separate: spirits 1 second, skeletons 1.5, golems 2. Deaths do not reset casting progress or trigger individual respawns. Casting progress can finish in the air, but spawning waits until the summoner is grounded and released. Minions follow their owner within a 150-unit leash and are valid enemy targets. Changing gear removes old summons and resets casting; owner death or leaving a stage removes summons. Elemental grimoires trigger their ability every five minion attacks.

Mage uses staves. Priest uses holy symbols, keeps its enemy-damaging/ally-buffing aura, and retains 80–90 base AGI. Weapons, fixed portraits, shop names, class choices, and stat readouts reflect the new roster.
