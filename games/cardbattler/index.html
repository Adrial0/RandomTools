How to play

Buy cards from the shop with gold, then select a card and an empty tile in your bottom three rows to deploy for free. Agents can deploy farther forward.

Drag a hand card over a compatible hand card. Hold until the circular indicator fills, then release on that card to merge. Releasing early, leaving the target, or dropping elsewhere cancels the merge.

End your turn to activate your army, then the enemy. Units attack forward or move one tile. Your army acts front to back, left to right; the enemy mirrors this.

Defenders in a base row protect their column. Defeat five enemy bases to complete the run. You have three lives; each lost battle costs one. Every battle starts with full base health and no carried-over cards.

Start with 60 gold. Gain 10 gold each round, plus 1 per living Miner or Businessman. The shop refreshes free each round; reroll costs 10 gold. Base cards cost 10 gold, two-color cards 30, four-color cards 50. Ability numbers are provisional.
